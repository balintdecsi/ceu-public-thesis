Business decision

The decision this work supports

Product question

Can ingatlan.com provide a credible rent suggestion during listing upload so landlords price competitively and reduce time-on-market?

The capstone focuses on offline modelling and recommendation support, not production deployment or A/B testing.

Model framing

  • Scope: Budapest rental flats.
  • Target: monthly rent per square metre in HUF.
  • Metric: MdAPE — typical absolute % miss.
  • Success criterion: ≤10% MdAPE, set by the client.
  • Guardrails: coverage, district stability, subtype stability, and future time-on-market linkage.

Market movement

Why temporal discipline matters

Observed pattern

  • Budapest rental HUF/sqm rose sharply after 2021 in the refreshed modelling sample.
  • The reserved 2025-11-29 → 2026-06-02 test period has a higher median price level than training.
  • Calendar and macro effects should not be learned from the future.
Implication: the model should be judged on out-of-time behaviour and district/subtype slices, not only average CV score.

Model comparison

Comparable CV leaderboard

Same target, same rolling time-series CV, same metric. Lower MdAPE is better.

What this says

  • Top candidates are close: XGBoost, HistGradientBoosting, and LightGBM all sit around 9.8–9.9% CV MdAPE, clearing the client’s 10% criterion in CV.
  • OLS and Ridge are surprisingly competitive at ~10.1%; they are useful as transparent benchmarks.
  • The strongest improvement is versus simple baselines: district median is ~16.8% and dummy median is ~17.9%.
Interpretation: a complex model helps, but the incremental gain over a well-specified linear/hedonic model is modest in CV. Stability and product fit matter.

SHAP beeswarm: which features push predictions up or down

How to read it

  • Each dot is one sampled listing; horizontal position is the SHAP contribution to the predicted log HUF/sqm.
  • Dots to the right increase the model prediction; dots to the left decrease it.
  • Colour shows whether that feature value is high or low for the listing.
  • Use this for transparency and sanity checks, not as a causal statement.
Product use: the same logic can support a local “what drives this estimate” explanation for a landlord-facing rent suggestion.

Feature engineering

Interaction terms kept in the model

The final feature policy avoids high-cardinality categorical interaction keys; it keeps a small set of numeric terms with clear formulas.

TermFormulaInterpretation
distance_x_log_areadistance_to_city_center_km × ln(area_size)Centrality gradient can differ for small vs. large flats.
has_balcony_x_distance_to_center1[balcony_size > 0] × distance_to_city_center_kmBalcony premium may vary with centrality.
elevator_x_floor_num1[has_elevator] × floor_numHigher floors are valued differently when an elevator exists.
total_room_equiv_x_log_area(room_count + 0.5 × small_room_count) × ln(area_size)Layout usefulness interacts with flat size.
room_count_x_log_arearoom_count × ln(area_size)Room count can matter differently by size.
balcony_to_area_ratioclip(balcony_size / area_size, 0, 0.5)A bounded ratio, not simple multiplication.
room_density(room_count + 0.5 × small_room_count) / area_sizeCaptures compactness of the layout.
Why this matters: these terms let the model represent plausible housing-market effects without exploding the one-hot feature space.

Product implications

Implications for the upload flow

Fields worth prioritizing

  • Area and rooms / room density.
  • Condition, comfort, subtype, heating.
  • Balcony size relative to area.
  • Air conditioning and equipment flags.
  • High-quality location: district, zone, coordinates/street enough for local comparable signal.

Potential UX pattern

  • Predict HUF/sqm, multiply by area for total-rent suggestion.
  • Show a range rather than a point estimate if uncertainty is high.
  • Suppress suggestion when coverage or district/subtype reliability is poor.
  • Optionally show “what drives this estimate” using SHAP-style local explanation.

Recommendation

Recommended next steps with ingatlan.com

1 · Freeze evaluation

Run notebook 22 as a controlled one-shot holdout, then publish final MdAPE/MAPE/RMSE, total-rent errors, coverage, and district/subtype diagnostics.

2 · Define UX bands

Use the client’s 10% MdAPE criterion as the offline target, then decide how it maps to product behaviour: exact suggestion, range suggestion, or no suggestion.

3 · Pilot safely

Start with Budapest rental flats only, monitor input coverage and prediction drift, and use the artifact’s feature list to improve upload-field completion.

Bottom line: the project produced a credible, reproducible offline model artifact and a practical roadmap. The remaining decision is product governance, not more exploratory modelling.

Appendix D — feature policy and leakage controls

Model feature policy

Included

  • Core property structure: area, rooms, floor, age.
  • Quality/amenity fields: condition, comfort, subtype, furnishment, heating, balcony, aircon.
  • Location: district and zone level 1 one-hot, coordinates, distance to center.
  • Fold-safe 90-day neighborhood prior and local deltas.
  • Lagged WorldPop and NDVI features.

Excluded / constrained

  • price_per_sqm_huf is the target, never a feature.
  • Total price_huf is not a feature.
  • Street/geo-cell identifiers are not directly one-hot encoded.
  • Text bodies are not used; only language-presence flags.
  • Model-specific filters stay downstream of the gold warehouse.
